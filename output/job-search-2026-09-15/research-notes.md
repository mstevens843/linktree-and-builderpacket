Research completed September 15, 2026, for Mathew Stevens, Pittsburg, California.

Scope: 20 new employers, California-eligible remote or Bay Area hybrid only. No applications, employer messages, or contact attempts were made. Application pages were read without entering candidate details or submitting forms.

The automated ATS candidate pool contained 7,788 distinct posting URLs across 206 normalized company slugs before the later manual additions. This was a discovery pool, not a claim that every URL was eligible or that every description received detailed manual review. More than 70 individual descriptions were reviewed in detail, followed by live application checks for the finalists. The final shortlist and exclusions are in recommendations.json and company-exclusions-for-next-round.json. Raw descriptions, source code reads, browser form observations, prior-round reconstruction, and rejected candidates are preserved in research-evidence.tar.gz.

The prior exclusions were reconstructed from the conversation, the September 14 session records, and the user's latest explicit list. The prior-company-exclusions.json file contains 110 names and aliases, not 110 necessarily distinct employers. The next-round file adds all 20 recommendations and their aliases. Companies merely investigated and rejected were not added to recommendations.

Background assessment:

- Socket Mobile, April 2020 to July 2023, was analytics, tagging QA, experiments, dashboards, and stakeholder reporting. It is not software engineering tenure. The reported sales growth was a contribution, not a result attributable solely to Mathew.
- Agentic and SolPulse have live product and app-store evidence. The web/SDK/native source supports product ownership, TypeScript/React/Node/Postgres work, native bridges, transaction approval, polling, simulation, and diagnostics. SolPulse's backend is private and was not independently audited.
- User counts, review totals, transaction concurrency, and monitoring speed are self-reported. They were not used as independently validated performance measurements in the ranking.
- Godot PRs 449, 453, and 454 and Unity PR 275 are authored upstream contributions. DBOS 838 and AgentCI 160 involved Mathew's investigations/reproductions; other contributors authored the merged implementation. Do not conflate those forms of contribution.
- Capability Record Replay, Eval Foundry, Crashpoint, and related projects provide research/prototype/evaluation evidence. They are not evidence of running a commercial ML training platform or serving enterprise AI customers.
- Newly inspected Crashpoint work includes recorded CrewAI tool-retry trials and CI artifacts. This is controlled local research, not production incident remediation. The 90/90 result concerns agreement with predictions over three controlled cases, not a universal reliability claim.
- The current Agentic repository contains Rust desktop wallet signing, key/state handling, and restart integration tests. signer.rs commit b64b58c244fbbbe40bbaa1c44d0a0c2fdbaf489a is attributed to mstevens843. The Android Java WebLaunchActivity validates deep-link scheme, host, and port. These establish limited hands-on Rust/Java evidence, not years of specialist experience.
- Main gaps: engineering tenure, enterprise customer books/presales, production Go/Python depth, Java/Spring, Kubernetes/cloud operations at scale, formal visual design work, and database administration. No organizational leadership was inferred from founder status.

Verification limits: open means that the official description and application workflow were available during this review, not a guarantee that an employer will continue hiring. US-wide remote listings were accepted when official material established US remote employment and no state exclusions appeared; an explicit California/SF pay band was additional evidence where available. Unspecified hybrid days were reported as unspecified. Compensation was not used to increase rankings.
