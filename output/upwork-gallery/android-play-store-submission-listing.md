## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Can a new personal Play account use this package?**

Yes, after verification, any required closed testing, and production-access approval for the app are complete. Tester recruitment, managing closed tests, and applying for production access are separate from this submission package.

**Do you guarantee approval or a publication date?**

No. The deliverable is a signed release bundle and a production submission for review. Google controls approval and publication timing. The 7-day scope starts after the app, account eligibility, signing access, and assets are ready.

**Do I need my own Google Play developer account?**

Yes. You own the verified account, complete required agreements, grant invited Play Console access, and pay all fees. I use your account. Existing apps must have the appropriate upload/signing access available.

**What happens if Google rejects the app?**

One metadata/signing correction and resubmission requested within 30 days of initial submission is included. Product-code changes, policy-driven feature changes, SDK migrations, account appeals, and extra review rounds require separate scope.

**Which apps fit this package?**

Existing Kotlin or React Native Android apps that already work and build. Send build status and prior review feedback before ordering. App conversion, new features, store artwork, legal-document writing, and iOS submission are separate.
