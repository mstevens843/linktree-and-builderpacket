## Package scope

- One existing, functional iOS app, one release/version, and submission to Apple App Store through the client's account.
- Existing Swift or React Native iOS projects are considered after build review. The source must already build for iOS. Framework conversion and major SDK upgrades are separate.
- Versioning, bundle identifier, signing and provisioning, and generation of the iOS release build using the client's Apple Developer account.
- Up to three agreed core-flow checks on one physical iPhone plus a simulator where useful. No device compatibility matrix or full QA audit.
- One English-language listing populated with client-supplied text, store-ready screenshots, icon, support/privacy URLs, age/content information, and reviewer credentials.
- Technical assistance entering App Privacy answers based on the client's confirmed data practices and disclosed SDKs. Client confirms all business, legal and data-handling declarations.
- Upload the build and submit one release for store review. Provide submission confirmation, version/build details, configuration changes, and handoff instructions.
- Seven-day delivery refers to initial submission after all account, asset and app prerequisites are ready. Apple's processing, review, approval and publication timing are outside the service provider's control.
- One revision covers an in-scope metadata/signing correction and, if necessary, one resubmission requested within 30 days of initial submission. Product-code changes, entitlement-policy redesign, payment changes, rejection appeals, and further review rounds are separate.
- Apple Developer account must belong to the client, be active/verified, and have necessary agreements completed. App Store Connect access and permissions must be ready for build upload and submission.
- Client provides reviewer test access and required content, owns/has rights to the app, and pays account/provider fees. No tester recruitment, account lending, guaranteed acceptance, downloads, ranking, store artwork design, or legal-document writing.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**1. Does this include Android or Google Play?**

```text
This listing covers one iOS app release submitted to Apple App Store. Google Play submission requires a separate scope. You must provide a working iOS project; converting a web or Android app to iOS is a separate service.
```

**2. Do you guarantee approval or a publication date?**

```text
No. The deliverable is a prepared iOS build and submitted release. Apple controls approval and publication timing. The 7-day delivery starts after the app, assets, account permissions, and required agreements are ready.
```

**3. Do I need my own Apple Developer account?**

```text
Yes. You need active Apple Developer membership and must invite the necessary App Store Connect and signing access. You pay Apple's fees and complete enrollment and agreements. I submit through your account, not an account I own.
```

**4. What happens if the store rejects the app?**

```text
One metadata/signing correction and resubmission requested within 30 days is included. App-code changes, payment or privacy feature changes, account appeals, and additional review rounds need separate scope. Store acceptance is not guaranteed.
```

**5. What must I supply, and whose account is used?**

```text
Your active developer account, app source, listing text, icon, screenshots, support/privacy URLs, and reviewer access. You confirm disclosures and pay fees. I use invited access to your account. Store artwork and legal-document writing are separate.
```
