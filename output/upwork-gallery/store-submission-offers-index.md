# Separate store-submission offerings

These replace the combined app-store-submission draft for new Upwork entry. The original generic cover and draft are preserved.

| Offer | Full guide | Cover | Price | Delivery | Revisions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| iOS App Store Submission | [Guide](ios-app-store-submission-listing.md) | [PNG](ios-app-store-submission-cover.png) | $600 | 7 days | 1 |
| Android Google Play Submission | [Guide](android-play-store-submission-listing.md) | [PNG](android-play-store-submission-cover.png) | $600 | 7 days | 1 |

Both have one tier and one operating system. Initial submission is the deliverable. One in-scope metadata/signing correction and resubmission requested within 30 days of initial submission counts as the one revision. Store processing, review, approval, and publication timing are controlled by the store.

Android requires verified account and production access for the app before starting. iOS can include one internal TestFlight group; external beta testing is separate.

Maximum simultaneous projects: 1 per listing. This can permit two simultaneous orders across these two listings.

Exact prompts, structured JSON, and validated field lengths are saved in this directory. Both covers use built-in image generation. No Upwork listings were published by the assistant.
